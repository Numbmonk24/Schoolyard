# BrightSteps Educational Aids Website

A clean, professional website for selling educational aids and visual supports designed for children who learn differently (ADHD, autism, learning challenges, sensory needs, etc.).

## What's Included

- **index.html** – Homepage with hero, audience sections, featured products, and benefits
- **shop.html** – Full product catalog with category filters
- **about.html** – About the mission and approach
- **resources.html** – Free sample downloads section
- **contact.html** – Contact form
- **css/styles.css** – Complete responsive styling
- **js/main.js** – Basic interactivity (filters, mobile menu, add-to-cart feedback)

## How to Use

### Option 1: View Locally
1. Open the folder `educational-aids-website`
2. Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser
3. Navigate between pages using the menu

### Option 2: Host for Free (Recommended)
Upload the entire folder to any of these free hosts:

- **Netlify** (netlify.com) – Drag & drop the folder
- **Vercel** (vercel.com)
- **GitHub Pages**
- **Cloudflare Pages**

### Option 3: Connect Real Payments
To turn this into a real store:
1. Create products on **Teachers Pay Teachers** or **Etsy**
2. Or connect **Stripe**, **Gumroad**, or **Payhip** for digital downloads
3. Replace the “Add to Cart” buttons with real checkout links

## Customization Tips

- Change the site name “BrightSteps” to your preferred brand name
- Update product names, prices, and descriptions
- Replace the emoji placeholders with real product mockup images
- Add your real email address on the contact page
- Add more products by copying a product-card block in `shop.html`

## Design Notes

- Calm teal color palette suitable for educational / special needs audience
- Fully responsive (works on phones and tablets)
- Accessible structure and clear typography
- Professional but warm tone
